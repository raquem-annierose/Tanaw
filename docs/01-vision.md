# 01: Vision: Hangout Game

## One-liner

A cozy Filipino-inspired hilltop hangout where players chill at a café overlooking city lights, meet new people through matched conversations, and decorate the world with stickers they make themselves.

## Raw ideas (draft)

| Idea                    | What it could become                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sticker maker**       | Players design simple stickers in-game (shapes, colors, text, emoji-style icons), then stick them on a wall, their cup, or their table. Later: trade/gift stickers. |
| **Matching talking**    | "Kwentuhan" matching: sit at a 2-seat table, get paired with a random player, and get an icebreaker prompt card ("Best kanto food?"). Leave anytime.                |
| **Antipolo overlook**   | The main map: a hillside view of glowing city lights at night, winding road, jeep/trike parking, stalls, the café at the edge of the cliff.                         |
| **Café design ni Lara** | Lara owns the café look: layout, furniture, menu board, vibe. The café is the "home base" of the map.                                                               |

## Theme & mood

- **Setting:** Antipolo-style hilltop at night, city lights below.
- **Feel:** cozy, warm, lo-fi, safe. "Tambay after class/work."
- **Palette:** warm amber lights, deep navy sky, coffee browns, a little neon from sari-sari signs.
- **Sound:** lo-fi/OPM-style chill music, jeepney horns far away, crickets, café chatter.
- **Day/night:** default is golden hour → night (lights turn on).

## Core features (MVP = first playable)

1. Map: overlook + café + seating areas
2. Sit anywhere (benches, café chairs, cliff edge)
3. Kwentuhan tables: match with a random player + prompt cards
4. Sticker maker v1: pick shape + color + text, place on a sticker wall
5. Café menu: order a drink (cosmetic item you hold)

## Later

- Day/night cycle, rain mode 🌧️
- Emotes (wave, cheers with coffee)
- Photo spot / camera mode
- Trade stickers, sticker packs
- Own table/booth decoration
- Game passes (cosmetics only, no pay-to-win)

## Safety rules (non-negotiable)

- All player text (chat, sticker text, prompts typed by players) **must** go through Roblox's text filter (`TextService:FilterStringAsync`) before others see it.
- Matching is opt-in, players can leave or block any time.
- No personal info prompts (no "where do you live / school").
